Step 01 / Why now
🌊

Your moment is here

For years the music game was built so other people ate first. Labels, platforms, middlemen. You made the sound, they kept the bag.

Web3 flips that. It is a new way to put your music online where you own it, you get paid direct, and nobody can quietly cut you out. Africa is early, and early is where the real upside lives.

Step 02 / In plain words
🔗

What "on-chain" means

A blockchain is just a public notebook that everyone can see and nobody can secretly erase or edit. Once your name is next to your song in that notebook, it is proof, forever.

Real talk: putting your work "on-chain" means your ownership is written in permanent ink that the whole world can check. No label can claim it. No platform can delete it.
Step 03 / The come up
💰

What you actually gain

  • 👑Own your mastersYour release is yours, provable and permanent.
  • ⚡Get paid directFans pay you. Smart contracts split money instantly, no middleman.
  • 🔄Earn on the resaleSet it once and earn every time your work trades again.
  • 🌐Borderless moneyReceive value from anywhere on earth. No bank gatekeeping you.
  • ⭐Reputation that travelsEarn Respect that follows you across the whole ecosystem.
  • 🏆Real fundingGrants, prize pools and comps like the ZABAL Gamez, open to you.
Step 04 / Keep it real
🛡

Know the risks

The upside is real, but so is being smart. Quick truths:

  • 01Token values move up and down. Never put in rent money.
  • 02Scams exist. Never share your secret phrase, ever. Not with anyone.
  • 03Transactions are final. Double check before you confirm.
  • 04You hold your own keys, so keep them safe and backed up.
Surfboard has your back: guided steps, plain language, safe defaults, and a real community walking in with you. You are not doing this alone.
Step 05 / Your key
🎧

Your wallet is your backstage pass

A wallet is your ID, your bank, and your all-access pass rolled into one. It holds your keys, it receives your money, and it is how you sign in everywhere in web3.

That is it. No paperwork, no bank branch. You approve everything yourself, and it runs on Base, a network with tiny fees. Set it up once and you are in.
