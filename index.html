The most important lesson

First move: get your wallet

Before anything else, you need a wallet. Your wallet has an address, a long code that starts with 0x. That address is how you receive every kind of money you earn or win. No wallet, no bag. Think of it as your account number for the internet of money, and you are the only one who holds the key.

Pick a wallet
Base AppBest for Africa
  1. Install the Base App from your phone app store.
  2. Open it and sign up with your email or a passkey.
  3. Your wallet is created for you automatically. No 12 words to memorise.
  4. Tap Receive to see and copy your wallet address. That is what you share to get paid.
MetaMask
  1. Install MetaMask, the phone app or the browser add-on.
  2. Tap Create a new wallet and set a strong password.
  3. It shows you 12 secret words. Write them on paper and hide them. Never type them anywhere online.
  4. Confirm the words. Done. Tap your address at the top to copy it.
Keep it safe
  • 01Your password and your 12 secret words are the keys to your money. Never share them with anyone, ever.
  • 02No real company or person will ever ask for your secret words. Anyone who asks is trying to rob you.
  • 03Write the words on paper. Do not keep them in screenshots, photos, WhatsApp or email.
  • 04Always check the address before you send. Money sent cannot be undone.
