What "onchain" means

A blockchain is a public notebook that everyone can read and nobody can secretly erase or edit. Once your name is next to your work in that notebook, it is proof, forever.

Real talk: putting something onchain means the record of it is written in permanent ink that the whole world can check. No company can claim it. No platform can delete it.

Card 05 / Your key
🔑

Your wallet is your backstage pass

A wallet is your ID, your bank and your all-access pass rolled into one. It holds your keys, it receives your money, and it is how you sign in everywhere onchain.

That is it. No paperwork, no bank branch. You approve everything yourself, and it runs on Base, a network with tiny fees. Set it up once and you are in.

Before anything else, you need a wallet. It has an address, a long code starting with 0x. That is how you receive every kind of money you earn or win. Behind it sits a secret recovery phrase, 12 words that ARE the wallet. Address: share it. Phrase: never.

Pick a wallet
PhantomEasiest start
  1. Install Phantom from your phone app store or as a browser add-on.
  2. Open it and tap Create a new wallet.
  3. It shows a secret recovery phrase. Write it on paper and hide it. Never type it anywhere online.
  4. Confirm the phrase. Done. Tap your address at the top to copy it, that is what you share to get paid.
MetaMask
  1. Install MetaMask, the phone app or the browser add-on.
  2. Tap Create a new wallet and set a strong password.
  3. It shows 12 secret words. Write them on paper and hide them.
  4. Confirm the words. Done. Tap your address at the top to copy it.
One wallet, every door. The same wallet signs you into $ONGCHAINN, Zora, Farcaster, the ZABAL Gamez and The ZAO. Set it up once and every door is open.

Onchain, you are your own bank. That is the power and the responsibility. Nobody can freeze your money, and nobody can refund it either. So the rules are simple and they are not optional.

The rules
  • 01Your 12 words never leave the paper you wrote them on. No screenshots, no WhatsApp, no email, no "support".
  • 02No real company or person will ever ask for your secret words. Anyone who asks is trying to rob you.
  • 03Bookmark the real sites and only use your bookmarks. Scammers clone real platforms with one letter changed.
  • 04Slow down when anyone rushes you. "Claim in 10 minutes" is farming panic. Real opportunities survive you sleeping on them.
  • 05Read what your wallet asks you to sign. If a site you just met wants permission to spend your tokens, close it.
  • 06Random token appeared in your wallet? Do not touch it. Interacting is the trap.
Pro move: keep two wallets. A main one that holds your money and your work, and a day to day one for trying new sites. If the small one gets burned, your catalogue is safe.

Gas, and why Base

Every onchain action costs a small network fee called gas. On Base it is usually less than a text message. That is why Surfboard onboards you there: same security as Ethereum, fees you will not notice. Keep a little ETH on Base in your wallet for gas, the way you keep airtime on your phone.

Same network, both sides. Money sent on the wrong network can be lost. When someone pays you, tell them: "Base network". When you send, check the network too.

Three words that sound scary and are not.

  • 🪙TokenDigital money that lives onchain. You can earn it, send it, hold it, or swap it into Kwacha. ETH and USDC are tokens.
  • 🎪Coin (creator or content coin)A token tied to a person or a piece of work. On Base, a song, an artwork or an artist can have a coin. People who believe in you buy it early. On $ONGCHAINN, songs are coins.
  • 🎨NFTOnchain proof that you own one specific thing: a track, a cover, a ticket, a name.
  • 🤖Smart contractA deal written in code that pays out by itself. Nobody can dodge it or change it later.
  • 🆕MintTo publish something onchain for the first time. Mint a song and its ownership record is born.
The point: the file does not go "inside" the blockchain. The proof does, like a title deed for a house. Your work stays yours even if every app disappears tomorrow.

Old internet vs new internet

Web2 is the internet you already use: Spotify, YouTube, Instagram, your bank app. It works, but you are renting space on platforms that belong to someone else. Web3 is the internet where you own your space. Same phone, same you, new rules.

In web2, your money passes through a label, a distributor and a platform before a slice reaches you, usually months later. Onchain, a smart contract split does the sharing the second money arrives.

Say you and your producer agree 70/30. You set that split once when you publish. Every sale, tip or resale after that pays out automatically: 70 to your wallet, 30 to theirs, in seconds, with the math public for both of you to check.

Resale royalties are the sleeper benefit. If a collector resells your work for more later, a percentage you chose comes back to you. Your old work keeps paying you as it grows.

Try it: when you upload on $ONGCHAINN or coin on Zora, look for the splits or royalties setting before you publish. Set it once, and it is locked in code.

Coining a song or an artwork means creating an onchain record that says: this exact thing exists, and this wallet made it. The file itself does not go "inside" the blockchain. The proof of it does, like a title deed for a house.
